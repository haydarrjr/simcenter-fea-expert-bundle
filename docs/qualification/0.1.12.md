# 0.1.12 mesh and contact qualification

This qualification follows the [0.1.12 implementation plan](../mesh-contact-implementation-plan-0.1.12.md). Native observations use Designcenter 2612.1700. The solver and BatchPost are the installed Siemens tools. All fixtures use independent synthetic geometry and inputs. Source documentation was resolved against the available 2606 manuals and checked against 2612 behavior where observed.

## Regional mesh chain

The live Designcenter Open and Simcenter 3D Model Inspection profiles opened the same FEM. A body-scoped Selection Recipe resolved one of two same-named faces. A `FaceDensitySize` control accepted the recipe itself. A fresh builder read it back and reported a size of 0.90 mm. The selected region's median quadratic tetrahedron corner edge decreased from 5.00 to 0.9091 mm. Native Element Quality evaluated 4 adjacent elements before remesh and 110 afterward. The measured applicable tests reported zero warnings and zero errors. A fresh process repeated the setup and saved the CAD/FEM/SIM. The live FEM reopen recovered the same recipe, control, 110 elements and 0.9091 mm median edge.

After a CAD width change from 10 to 12 mm, the original name-based recipe resolved no faces. Reidentification by the owning body and physical end restored the one 48 mm² face and the local mesh control. Dividing that CAD face resolved two 24 mm² children. The stored control contained both the recipe and an extra child face after synchronization. The independent target read identified the extra scope. Rebinding to the recipe and reopening the full SIM recovered only the recipe target, the two child faces, 148 adjacent elements and a median edge of 0.8571 mm. The two faces remained on the intended body. Reading the FEM alone left the child CAD geometry unresolved in one live read.

Moving a body-scoped bounding box changed the selection from both split end faces to the opposite face. An intentionally displaced Tet10 midside node changed the solver checker Jacobian witness from positive to negative and triggered three tests while the corner-based aspect value stayed fixed. Restoring the node coordinates restored the Jacobian witness and cleared the element failures. The live simulation reopened with the same CAD/FEM/SIM hashes before and after these observations.

A separate CAD-backed live FEM exercised cylinder density and local fillet-face sizing on Tet10 meshes. Cylinder median curved-surface edge length decreased from 1.3333 to 0.7059 mm and maximum corner-chord sag from 0.05456 to 0.00856 mm. The fillet median edge decreased from 0.8572 to 0.3735 mm and maximum corner-chord sag from 0.10926 to 0.01482 mm. On the measured surface edges, projected midside nodes remained within 0.000004 mm of the cylindrical radius. Chord sag describes the linear corner-to-corner approximation. The curved quadratic surface has its own mapping and Element Quality checks.

## Open–closed–open contact

The [SOL 401 fixture](../../tests/fixtures/sol401-elastic-contact-cycle.txt) and [SOL 402 fixture](../../tests/fixtures/sol402-elastic-contact-cycle.txt) prescribe four displacement states. The independent nominal closed-force reference is 250 N at 0.075 mm and 500 N at 0.100 mm. Both runs give a 0.025 mm final clearance at 0.025 mm top displacement and near-zero contact force. The pre-contact and reopened states are independently distinguishable from the loaded closed states.

Both fine-increment trials bracketed the analytical closure at 0.050 mm. Normal contact force was below 1 N at that state. The next accepted state at 0.053125 mm carried 29.76 N in SOL 401 and 31.25 N in SOL 402. After unloading, the following accepted state at 0.0484375 mm had positive separation and force below 1 N. Separate fresh solves of the native re-exported decks reproduced the baseline F06 forces, separation and displacement. [Contact cycle verification](../../tests/verify_contact_fixtures.py)

Live readback from the saved and reopened SIMs confirmed each FEM, four structural steps, manual surface-to-surface contact and a byte-matched OP2 attachment. Native displacement at the end of each subcase agreed with the prescribed history. BatchPost required explicit meter, kilogram and second source units for the standalone OP2. Service gap and pressure matched F06, including SOL 401's −0.002381 mm and 4.7619 MPa and SOL 402's zero separation and 5.0000 MPa. SOL 401 provides numeric contact status in OP2. The observed SOL 402 file exposes separation and pressure for the same physical determination.

## Hertz benchmark

The [fixture generator](../../tests/fixtures/generate_hertz_contact.py) builds quarter-symmetric CHEXA8 sphere-on-block cases in SI units. Run it with solver, radius division and half-width in meters. The primary series uses divisions 4, 8 and 16 at 0.010 m and repeats division 16 at 0.018 m. The reported loads and resultants represent the whole geometry. The radius uses active nodal area weights within facets. Center pressure uses the source node on the symmetry axis. Approach uses the loaded center of the remote top surface and the constrained far surface.

At 100 N, the fine-grid normalized pressure-profile RMSE was 0.0429 for SOL 401 and 0.0488 for SOL 402. Support reaction matched the applied load and penetration divided by analytical approach was 0.00218 and effectively zero, respectively. In both solvers the medium-to-fine and larger-boundary radius and pressure differences met the planned convergence limits. The fine-grid center-pressure errors exceeded the 5% limit. The approach errors were −7.18% and −7.37%, also outside the 5% limit. The qualification status is **PARTIAL** for both Hertz studies.

Each fine case was imported and saved as a native FEM/SIM. The live SIM read resolved the associated FEM, active force-step and surface pair. Native displacement at 100 N was −3.93244 μm and −3.92457 μm for SOL 401 and SOL 402, respectively, from the exact solver OP2 attachment. The native source-center pressure, the BatchPost SI-bound CSV and F06 agreed within 0.001 MPa for both sequences.
