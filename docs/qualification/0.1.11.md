## Associative SOL 101

The CAD master drives a solid FEM and SOL 101 SIM. In the live session, the driving length changed from 60 to 90 mm. The FEM update changed the mesh from 244 to 246 quadratic tetrahedra. Independent readback confirmed that the same support remained at the fixed end and the same pressure load followed the moving end. Both exported decks solved, with signed axial load and reaction of −100 N and +100 N. BatchPost recovered mean axial displacement on the four end corners of −0.0002882905 mm initially and −0.0004331198 mm after the change. The displacement difference matched the constant-section compliance increment within 0.10%. The 90 mm variant is retained as the exact live-exported deck. The native files retain the 60 mm baseline. The live check restored the original open-part inventory and work/display roles.

## SOL 401 contact and bolt preload

The installed Siemens Nastran 2D plane-stress bolt/contact regression (`n401cgb2d002.dat`) provides the reference deck and solver comparison. The input was imported, saved, reopened and re-exported. Live readback confirmed the SIM, linked FEM, preload and service steps, contact objects and exact attached OP2 reference. The F06 results agree with the independently run reference deck. The selected bolt reached the specified installation force of 100 MN and showed 543.4094 MN at the final service state. The reported lock-in strain stayed at 0.001964465. At the final state, the inactive witness had positive separation and was absent from the pressure and contact force tables. The active witnesses carried compressive pressure with finite penalty penetration. BatchPost recovered axial force, contact status, separation and pressure from the bound OP2 and the values agreed with F06. The bolt table repeats the bolt force across the reported elements.

The reference covers a force-controlled preload followed by sequential service with edge contact. The seed deck is supplied with the solver installation. [Bolt preload method](../../skills/fea-connector-contact/references/engineering-method.md)

## SOL 402 internal restart

The [elastic restart fixture](../../tests/fixtures/sol402-internal-restart.txt) restores the first subcase after an unrelated intermediate subcase. The exported deck retained `SEQDEP=NO` and the selected `RSUB`. The F06 positions the restore at source time 1 s and continues the target sequence from the prior end time of 2 s. At the first accepted increment, time 2.25 s, axial displacement is 0.060 mm. The live NXOpen result read matched that value. With only `RSUB` removed, a fresh start at the same target increment gives 0.015 mm. Both reach 0.060 mm at 3 s. The first accepted increment is the discriminator. The attached result exposes an additional output frame at 2 s, which reported zero displacement in this run. Use the restoration diagnostic and first accepted increment to establish the resumed state.
