# Real 3D transient fields and downstream durability

Bind the actual Simcenter/Nastran solution, mesh, units, spatial loading patterns, time grid, initial conditions, damping and solver output states. Use exact-release solver documentation and qualified results. From BatchPost or an advertised native extraction capability obtain the actual signed stress/strain history at the requested element/integration point and frame, together with displayed time and component identities.

Preserve a common physical interval when comparing refined time grids. Compare displacement and stress histories on compatible samples, including direct peak/reversal checks and phase errors. Treat sparse or irregular outputs explicitly. Good energy balance alone cannot establish the right peaks, phase or number of reversals. Record timestep, save interval, relative tolerances and how interpolation was bounded. Do not use repeated coarse output as a second refinement level.

Check force/moment and energy components with the solver's sign convention, including damping and applied work. Use a compatible mass/stiffness bound or time-integration check where possible. Preserve the physical time, integration location, coordinate axes, shear convention and original field values before passing history to [durability](../../fea-durability/SKILL.md). A spatial subset can retain all times but cannot establish a global hotspot.
