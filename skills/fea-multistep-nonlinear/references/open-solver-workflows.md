# Nonlinear multistep load-path verification

Define the response and a wrong-but-plausible model before assessing the solve. For a locked bolt, a continuing force controller may reproduce initial preload while removing the intended stiffness change. A wrong contact pair may converge while transferring no load. Total strain can conceal altered plastic strain.

Use the effective Siemens deck and exact-release documentation for load inheritance, released constraints, step ordering and state transfer. Capture force versus adjustment control for every preload step, contact normal/sign and opposing regions, initial gap, friction, material law, temperature reference and the resolved units. Separate intended overlapping regions from shared nodes that cannot open/slip. Review the cut integration for section forces rather than assuming a scalar control reaction equals bolt force.

For each step, compare attained loads, internal and external force/moment, residuals, contact pressure/area, gap, slip and plastic strain or temperature where applicable. Inspect stabilization/contact work and time step sensitivity near transitions. When changing step size, mesh or model parameters retain the original comparison state and distinguish convergence from physical model adequacy. Use compatible simple reference cases, such as a bolt/joint compatibility calculation or uniaxial return consistency within its assumptions.

## Uniaxial plasticity and load reversal

For monotonic tension with linear isotropic work hardening, use `ε = σ/E + εp` and `σ = σy + Hεp` after initial yield. Here `H` is the stress slope versus plastic strain. For elastic unloading before reverse yield, keep the accumulated plastic strain constant and recover strain with the elastic slope. Verify boundary conditions permit lateral contraction in a uniaxial stress reference. A laterally restrained fixture needs a different multiaxial reference.

For SOL 106 resolve `NLPARM` on each subcase and request intermediate output and `NLSTRESS` when nonlinear stress and accumulated plastic strain are needed. Check that the actual MATS1 definition retains the yield field when using bilinear hardening by `H`. The Support Center documents a missing `LIMIT1` export defect in earlier releases. Check the current effective deck before applying a release-specific workaround. [MATS1](https://docs.sw.siemens.com/en-US/doc/PL20251024315120185.QRG/html/id508406?audience=external) · [KB000153430](https://support.sw.siemens.com/en-US/knowledge-base/KB000153430_EN_US) · [Community diagnosis](https://community.sw.siemens.com/s/question/0D54O00008ACs36SAD)

Read actual attained loads and accepted increments. A cumulative load-factor label can continue across subcases even while the applied force decreases. Compare signed force balance and total, elastic or plastic response using the requested field and recovery position. Strain tensor components and accumulated equivalent plastic strain serve different checks.

For the observed standalone SOL 402 route on 2612, an OP2 produced with `PARAM,POST,-1` opened with an empty BatchPost catalog despite completed and valid F06 output. A separately exported and solved deck with `PARAM,POST,-2` exposed displacement, reactions, engineering stress and effective plastic strain for all expected steps. Qualify this setting against the target release and result reader. Check the actual deck, the catalog and signed values. A change to output format does not by itself validate the model or the solve.
