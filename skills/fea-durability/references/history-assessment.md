# Signed histories, nonlinear notch response and life interpretation

Verify coordinates and all tensor components before choosing a fixed uniaxial direction. Obtain the full signed linearly elastic stress history at the selected location and a relevant material S–N curve, including surface, mean stress, environment, temperature and reliability basis as applicable. Use a durable record of solution, time, point, frame and raw history hash.

Estimate cycle counts with an explicitly qualified rainflow convention. Preserve residual half cycles and the distinction between range and amplitude. For Miner damage sum n_i/N_i only with a compatible mean-stress correction and S–N data. Verify closed loops with a small independent history. Use appropriately qualified fatigue capabilities for nonlinear notch response, welded joints, rotating-principal or general multiaxial loading. For an FKM elastic-notch route, confirm the elastic input assumption and obtain roughness, gradient, surface, notch and reliability parameters. Keep hysteresis and alternative FKM parameters separate where supported.

Inspect fatigue-relevant stress and time resolution, hotspot selection coverage and mesh convergence. A Miner sum below one, a clipped transient history or extrapolated nominal life cannot by itself establish acceptance.
