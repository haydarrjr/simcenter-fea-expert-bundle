# Simcenter Nastran random response

Use the [engineering method](engineering-method.md) to establish the linear state, PSD/CSD convention, band, PSD matrix validity and independent RMS integral. SOL 111 can provide deterministic transfer responses and a random response. Check the exact Case Control and requested output to identify their scopes.

- Reconcile each intended independent load or enforced-motion pattern with its frequency-response subcase and amplitude normalization. Resolve the applicable frequency set, damping, `RANDOM` selector, `RANDPS` J/K pair and `TABRND1` table. Check real/imaginary scaling, units, interpolation, support band and extrapolation according to the installed solver. Compare the selected output requests to the intended subcase, component and recovery.
- For tabular import, each spectrum needs reference and response channel identities, quantities, units, frames and frequency samples. Validate unique channels/points, monotonic finite grids and PSD/CSD admissibility before authoring. Form the conjugate triangle if the schema supplies only one triangle. Use zero cross terms only when the independence assumption is explicit. Reserve and independently read back subcase/field mapping for any installed builder workflow.
- Check `Syy = H Sff Hᴴ` at sampled frequencies and integrate the correct one-sided band for RMS. A reported RMS, a rate of crossings and a frequency-resolved PSD require their own result witness. For tensor-derived output verify covariance and the solver's invariant convention. Do not calculate equivalent random stress by treating separate RMS components as one simultaneous tensor.
- Use the [Nastran text observer](../../simcenter-nastran/SKILL.md) and original F06 for case-card lineage. Use BatchPost or an appropriate qualified native reader for actual response exports. A fresh solver run, native persistence and engineering acceptance are separate observations.
