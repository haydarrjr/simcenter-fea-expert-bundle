# Body and material inspection

Use `body-info-tool` and `material-info-tool` in the bound Model Inspection profile. Apply `bodyName` or `materialName` when scope calls for it. Record the ownership, assignment (body or mesh), available material properties and units. Cross-check mesh and property tables when a material record exists without the intended assignment. Use [engineering material checks](../fea-materials-properties/SKILL.md) to evaluate the constitutive data.
