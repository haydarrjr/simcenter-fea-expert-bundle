# Designcenter Open connection

Use the host's Designcenter Open profile and its `dc_connection_status` on first binding or a connection symptom. An attached profile may expose a delayed catalog. Refresh through the host after a connection change and confirm the tool required by the task.

Distinguish missing registration, disconnected transport, connected session without an active part, and an operation error. Follow the [shared recovery policy](../fea-sc3d-tool-routing/SKILL.md). Use the Designcenter profile independently for CAD and NXOpen tasks.
